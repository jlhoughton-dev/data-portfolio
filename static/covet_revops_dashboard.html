Signals worth investigating

Illustrative talking points, not causal conclusions
Revenue is concentrated in larger accounts

Enterprise accounts contribute a meaningful share of MRR. Retention and expansion within this segment may have outsized revenue impact.

Integration coverage is high

Most clinics are connected to a PMS. Compare activation and usage for connected versus unconnected clinics to test whether integration is associated with adoption.

Usage deserves a closer look

Review the daily trend for weekday patterns, outliers, and any sustained decline. Pair usage with account tenure and customer tier before drawing conclusions.

Data note: This is synthetic sample data. The source contains repeated clinic-level fields on daily rows, so MRR and clinic counts are calculated from one record per clinic, while scribe sessions are aggregated across daily activity. Every invoice status is marked Paid, so this dataset cannot support payment-failure or collection-rate analysis. The current date may represent a partial day.
