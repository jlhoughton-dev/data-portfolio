Signals worth investigating

Observations, questions, and possible next steps
Acquisition volume is not channel attribution

Signup dates show when clinics entered the dataset, but there are no campaign, source, or lead fields. Next step: connect CRM and campaign data before judging channel efficiency.

Low recent usage is a follow-up signal

Recent sessions per seat can help identify clinics worth checking in with. Validate onboarding stage, team size, and product context before interpreting low activity as risk.

Integration is a product question to test

Compare adoption across integration states, then investigate with a larger and more representative dataset. This synthetic sample cannot establish that an integration causes higher usage.

Data note: This is synthetic sample data. Clinic metrics use one record per clinic; usage uses one record per clinic per event date (2,275 clinic-day records); invoices are kept at clinic + invoice-date grain (429 records). All 429 invoice records are marked Paid, and the source notes invoice amounts/statuses are not a validated billing ledger. Signup dates are available, but campaign/source attribution, lead stages, customer churn, support tickets, feature-level events, and product feedback are not present. Those business areas are therefore represented only through available proxies, not presented as measured outcomes.
Prepared as an interview portfolio example · Cross-functional views use only fields available in the synthetic sample and should be validated against production definitions before business use.
