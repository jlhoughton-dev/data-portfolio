# Portfolio Overview

I work at the intersection of data, operations, and business decision-making. My portfolio focuses on building the data foundations that help teams understand performance, capacity, and operational health—then turn that understanding into action.

This work spans data architecture, SQL modeling, reporting, and business intelligence. I am especially interested in the gap between operational data and executive decision-making: creating the systems, logic, and narratives that make complex information usable for real-world decisions.

## What I do

I design and deliver work in three connected areas:

- Data modeling and transformation
- Workforce and operational analytics
- Executive-facing reporting and insight communication

Across each area, the goal is the same: make underlying data reliable, interpretable, and actionable.

## How I approach work

My approach is grounded in clarity, structure, and business context.

- I start with the business question and operational context
- I build clean, maintainable data pipelines and transformations
- I validate the logic behind the metrics
- I translate the results into insights leaders can use

This combination of technical rigor and business understanding is what shapes my work.

## What this portfolio demonstrates

This portfolio highlights projects and models built to answer meaningful operational questions, including:

- workload and staffing analysis
- support capacity and performance evaluation
- revenue and operational impact reporting
- data architecture patterns for trustworthy reporting

The goal is not only to show technical depth, but also to demonstrate that the analysis supports decisions, not just dashboards.

## Closing thought

I believe the most valuable data work is the work that helps people understand what is happening, why it matters, and what should happen next.
