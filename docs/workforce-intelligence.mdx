<div className="workforceSectionCard">

## 🧭 Situation

Workforce and operational data was fragmented and difficult to interpret at the team level. Leaders had access to employee and performance data, but not a consistent way to understand whether staffing levels were aligned with workload demand.

This made it difficult to identify how workload was distributed, where capacity risk was emerging, and how staffing structure affected performance.

</div>

<div className="workforceSectionCard">

## 🎯 Task

The goal was to create a reliable analytical foundation that connected employee structure, workload, and performance in one consistent view.

The model needed to help leaders evaluate staffing health, understand capacity constraints, and support more informed operational decisions.

</div>

<div className="workforceSectionCard">

## 🛠️ Action

I built a structured data model that brought together the most relevant workforce signals.

This included:

- mapping employee hierarchy and manager relationships
- standardizing workload and portfolio metrics
- evaluating staffing patterns against demand and coverage
- creating reporting logic for operational analysis
- organizing the model into a dependable reporting layer

The focus was on making the data consistent, explainable, and useful in a business context.

</div>

<div className="workforceSectionCard">

## 📈 Result

The result was a more transparent and decision-ready view of workforce capacity and performance.

Leaders could better understand:

- where capacity was stretched
- where staffing was under- or over-allocated
- which operational signals were driving performance
- where management attention should be focused

This supported better conversations around staffing, workload balance, and operational readiness.

</div>
