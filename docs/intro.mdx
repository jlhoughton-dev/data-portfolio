# Portfolio Overview

I work at the intersection of data, operations, and business decision-making. I build the data foundations that help teams understand performance, capacity, and operational health, then turn that understanding into action.

## Focus Areas

- Data modeling and transformation
- Workforce and operational analytics
- Executive-facing reporting and insight communication

## Working Approach

My approach combines technical rigor with business context:

- I start with the business question and operational context
- I build clean, maintainable data pipelines and transformations
- I validate the logic behind the metrics
- I translate the results into insights leaders can use

## What You Will Find Here

The case studies show how raw relational data becomes modular SQL models, workforce analysis, and executive reporting. Each project connects the technical implementation to the operational question it is designed to answer.
